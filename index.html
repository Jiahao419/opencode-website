My Favorite Games

Gaming has always been more than a hobby for me. It's a way to unwind, explore rich worlds, and connect with stories and characters that stick with me long after the credits roll. Whether I'm grinding through a JRPG or wandering an open landscape, the escape and creativity gaming offers keep me coming back.

Top Three Favorite Games

Persona 5

A stylish JRPG about a group of students who change hearts as the Phantom Thieves. The turn-based combat, social simulation, and unforgettable soundtrack make every playthrough a treat.

Genshin Impact

An open-world action RPG with elemental combat and breathtaking scenery across Teyvat. Exploring its regions and teaming up friends for co-op keeps it endlessly replayable.

Hollow Knight

A hand-drawn 2D metroidvania set in the fallen kingdom of Hallownest. Its challenging bosses, hidden lore, and atmospheric world make it one of my all-time favorites.

What I Enjoy About Gaming

  • Immersive stories and memorable characters.
  • Exploring beautiful, hand-crafted worlds.
  • Learning mechanics and overcoming hard challenges.
  • Playing solo or sharing the experience with friends.
